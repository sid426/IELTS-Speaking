# Answer Generation Rules

This file contains the detailed IELTS speaking answer-generation rules for this repo.

## Shared Rules

- Audience fit: answers should sound like a Chinese 18-35-year-old speaker in everyday life.
- English should sound spoken and natural, not stiff or essay-like.
- Any score-raising expression must be bold in the English answer and must also appear in the material list.
- Qualifying score-raising expressions include idioms, semi-fixed expressions, phrasal verbs, multi-word topic nouns, advanced verbs, advanced adjectives/adverbs, evaluative or emotional expressions, and reusable discourse markers when they genuinely function as answer language.
- Chinese translation should be faithful, natural, and local.
- Highlighted English expressions should correspond to highlighted Chinese expressions where appropriate.
- Vocabulary lists must be built only from expressions that actually appear in the answer body.
- If a `Verbs`, `Nouns`, or `Adjectives` category has no valid item, leave that category visually blank instead of inventing filler.

## Part 2

### Tone And Goal

- Goal: Band 8 answer for Chinese 18-35-year-old speakers.
- Ground the content in daily life.
- Keep the tone light-hearted.
- Mild self-deprecating humor is allowed if it stays gentle and safe.

### Question

- List the question here.

### English Answer

- Exactly 4 paragraphs.
- Each paragraph 3 to 5 sentences.
- Around 200 to 230 words total.
- First person by default unless the card clearly requires another perspective.
- Conversational spoken English with strong phrasing and accurate grammar.

### Style

- Opening should naturally cover who / when / where or the card basics.
- Details should feel visual and specific.
- Everyday China-friendly details are welcome: delivery apps, metro, cafe queues, short trips, campus routines, and similar scenes.
- Humor should stay gentle and safe.

### Suggested Paragraph Logic

- Paragraph 1: who / when / where with a light hook.
- Paragraph 2: concrete actions and scenes.
- Paragraph 3: feelings, reactions, and a light self-deprecating moment.
- Paragraph 4: meaning, takeaway, or reflection.

### High-Score Material

- Bold all qualifying score-raising expressions in the English body first.
- Then build the lists from those bold items only.
- `Idiomatic Expressions`: at least 13 items.
- `Topic-related Vocabulary`: at least 4 `Verbs`, 4 `Nouns`, and 4 `Adjectives`.
- Do not duplicate items between idioms and topic vocabulary.

### Output Sections

Keep these sections exactly:

- `题目`
- `高分回答范例（Band 8）`
- `英文答案`
- `中文翻译`
- `答题逻辑框架`
- `高分语料`
- `Idiomatic Expressions`
- `Topic-related Vocabulary`

## Part 3

### Topic

- List the questions under the topic here.

### English Answer

- One paragraph.
- No more than 90 words.
- Natural and spoken, but still logically tight and idea-driven.

### Style

- Slightly deeper and more analytical than Part 1.
- Examples should still feel close to Chinese 18-35-year-old life.
- The first 1 to 2 sentences should ideally contain 1 to 2 simple hook-style idiomatic expressions.

### Logic Framework

Match the logic framework to the question type:

- opinion
- cause & effect
- compare & contrast
- hypothetical / future
- advantages & disadvantages
- problem-solution

### High-Score Material

- 3 to 5 idiomatic expressions.
- 4 to 6 topic-related vocabulary items total.
- Group vocabulary into `Verbs`, `Nouns`, and `Adjectives`.
- Never invent vocabulary entries that do not appear in the answer itself.
